About

I recently completed an MSc in Public Administration (International & European Governance) at Leiden University, following a BSc in European Studies at The Hague University of Applied Sciences. My research focuses on public opinion, EU foreign policy, and disinformation in Central Europe.

Before returning to formal education, I spent several years living and working across Denmark, the UK and Vietnam. That gave me an opportunity to experience different cultures and languages first-hand, which had a profound influence on who I am today — something an academic track alone couldn't have provided.
